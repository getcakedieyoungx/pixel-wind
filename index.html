    02 - Why it doesn't shimmer

    Most wind shaders rotate or skew the sprite and round every pixel on its own, so the inside of the canopy flickers. Pixel Wind moves whole leaf clumps by whole pixels instead. Every output pixel takes exactly one pixel from your sprite: 

    dst(x, y) = src(x − dx, y − dy)

    Neighbouring clumps can move by different amounts without tearing seams, and no colour is ever invented.

    03 - Checked on every export

    1. Frame 1 is byte-identical to your sprite
    2. No colours or transparency levels that aren't in your sprite
    3. Everything below the pin line never moves
    4. At most 1–3 px sideways and 1 px vertical
    5. The last → first step is as smooth as every other step
